Key decisions:

1. One hue per field family, not per field: identity slate, dating green, obstetric purple (shared by history and previous pregnancies since they are one clinical domain), medical teal, medications amber, so the Delivery Planning rainbow collapses into a calm, predictable rhythm.
2. Every label is dark-on-tint from the same family (all at or above 4.5:1) while values stay ink-dark 16px in a 10px-radius box, so heading (navy 15px bold), label (12px tinted) and value read as three distinct layers.
3. The dashboard card moves the status tag inline after the name (tag first in reading order, name flexes with RTL-aware alignment), which removes the dead band and drops resting height back to the documented ~110dp.
4. Risk and completeness always carry a word ("Moderate risk", "Incomplete", severe BP tag in Arabic) and the 4dp edge strip is only a secondary cue, never the sole signal.
5. Tappable rows (previous pregnancies, Edit actions) keep a 48px minimum touch height, and the whole mock uses exactly two radii, 10 and 20, with no gradients, shadows or decorative icons.

Note: this is a mockup deliverable, so I answered with the HTML directly rather than writing files.
