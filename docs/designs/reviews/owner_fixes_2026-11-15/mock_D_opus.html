Mock D: Overview fields and the patient card

Builds on mock C and takes three things from mock B. Mocked at 360 dp, light theme, with one dark variant. Sample data is limited to the owner's real sample: Mona Ragab, منى رجب, 27+2 wks, G4 P1 A2, 110/70, A+, EDD 11/02/2027, Mothercare, 12000. Every field the sample does not hold shows the app's own "Not recorded" state instead of invented history.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: an antenatal EMR record and patient list for doctors scanning between consultations, in a calm Material 3 clinical style, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1. Uniform section rhythm is deliberate here: a clinician learns one pattern once and finds every field in the same place on every patient.

Why each choice

  • Heading 18 > value 16 > label 12: three sizes, and three different weights (bold, regular, semibold) and colours (navy, ink, family). Fixes the scorer's two C defects: heading 15 smaller than value 16, and label and value both at weight 600.
  • Value regular, label semibold: the small coloured label is the anchor the eye finds, the larger value stays easy to read in long prose such as surgical history.
  • Four families instead of C's five: identity and dating answer "who and when", so they share one blue. Fewer hues stay under the 2 to 3 core colours plus accent cap (R-29) and stop the page reading as a rainbow.
  • Delivery card joins the same system with one plum family, replacing today's purple, green, blue and pink. The owner keeps the label over tinted box pattern he likes, without four hues.
  • Each section is its own outlined card on the grey page, so two obstetric sections side by side never merge.
  • Read-only boxes are 44dp minimum, tappable records 56dp with a visible "Edit" word, section actions 48 by 48dp. Height is spent only where a finger goes.
  • Prose fields are full width; only short values (blood group, GA, EDD, G P A) share a row. No half-width wrapping.
  • Patient card: the tag joins the name row, the 3.5dp side strip and the 4% red card tint go, because the tag word already says the state. Normal risk shows no tag, so a tag on the list always means "look here".
  • Card labels move from 10sp to 12sp and values from 14sp to 16sp. The card still gets shorter because the separate Incomplete row (48dp) is gone: 152 px against 181 px for today's Incomplete card, measured in this mock's browser render, not on a device. A Moderate risk card today is 127 px, so for that state the new card is taller, because its type rises to the 12sp and 16sp floors and blood group and due date get real cells.

Taken from mock C

  • The label above tinted value box pattern, the desaturated equal-lightness tints, the full-width "wide" field.
  • The two-row card grid: GA, Parity, BP, then Blood group and a wider Due date.
  • Native RTL mirroring on the Arabic card with numbers isolated as LTR. Mock D uses inline <bdi>, not a block dir="ltr", so Arabic labels and values both align right (C's alignment bug).

Taken from mock B

  • Each section contained in its own outlined card.
  • Previous pregnancies as full-width tappable records with a trailing Edit, now in the obstetric tint with 16sp values (B had dropped both).
  • 48 by 48dp action targets (C's Edit was about 28dp wide).

Rejected

  • B's summary chip above repeated G P A fields: G P A now appears once, as three fields.
  • C's invented sample history (age, appendicectomy, folic acid, years of losses): replaced by "Not recorded" or counts the G4 P1 A2 sample already implies.
  • Header icons in tinted tiles: they repeat the heading and are decoration. Uppercase delivery titles: tracked caps are an antislop tell, sentence case reads faster.
  • Coloured side strip on the patient card: it repeats the tag and is a left-stripe pattern with no extra meaning.
  • A "Normal risk" green tag on every quiet card: it would put colour on every row and dilute the real alerts.

Rules applied

  • antislop core: R-02 no em dashes, R-06 no tracked caps, R-11 two radii, R-17 and R-38 no invented data, R-25 computed contrast, R-29 palette cap, R-31 a written reason for each decision, dials E1 R1 M1 (hover and pressed states only).
  • antislop-ui: no left stripe, no decorative icons, no gradients, glow or shadows; shadows dropped from the card (elevation 0, outline only).
  • antislop-human: contrast computed with the WCAG formula, status never by colour alone, native buttons with names, visible focus ring, pointer cursor.
  • antislop-layoutmobile: 360dp first, no fixed-width children, ellipsis only on one-line card values, 48dp targets with gaps.
  • frontend-design: plan before build, one focal point per card (the name row), sentence-case copy in the user's words.
  • design-critique: each mock C and B defect from the blind score is answered in the notes under the frames.

Static mock: buttons show hover, pressed and focus states but do nothing, verified by reading the markup. Not checked on a device.

6. Self-critique at 360 px

  • Overflow: section content width is 360 minus 2 by 16 gutter minus 2 by 16 card padding, 296dp. Two columns are 144dp each, three columns 93dp; the longest short value "11/02/2027" at 16sp semibold is about 88dp, so nothing wraps. "Agreed delivery hospital" wrapped at half width in the first render, so both delivery fields are now full width.
  • Card: inner width 312dp minus the 48dp More button leaves 264dp for name and tag. "Moderate risk" is about 100dp, so a name gets about 155dp per line and two lines before ellipsis. Arabic "خطورة متوسطة" is narrower. Due date and delivery date span two columns, so "Not recorded" never truncates.
  • Contrast: every pair in the table passes 4.5:1 (lowest text pair 5.64, Not recorded on the delivery tint); tag borders pass 3:1.
  • Arabic: labels and values align right, the name sits at the right, tag and More at the left, and isolates keep A+ from rendering as +A.
  • Open point: on an English-locale phone with an Arabic name, the card stays LTR and only the name shapes RTL. That is the honest default; full RTL comes with the Arabic locale.
  • Open point: the delivered state shows the recorded G4 P1 A2; after a delivery the app's own parity update decides what the card shows.
