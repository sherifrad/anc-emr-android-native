Mock S07: Scans tab (band cards)

Third version, in the owner's chosen middle style (Option A): one card per scan, a teal band with chips and the date, a quiet body. Mona Ragab's sample has no scan, so her tab is empty. The populated card uses the in-repo demo fixture DemoFixtures scan ...0102 (2026-05-30, 20+0 wks, BPD 49, HC 178, AC 155, FL 34, EFW 380, FHR 148, Cephalic, Anterior, Normal fluid), labelled. The photo card is layout only.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: the scan record compared visit to visit, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

  • One container per scan with an Obstetric family band (teal, #E3F0EE): GA chip ("20+0 wks"), a "2 photos" chip when images exist, the date, Delete and Edit.
  • Quiet body: biometry in three plain columns (BPD, HC, AC, FL, EFW, FHR), anatomy in the same grid (Presentation, Placenta, Amniotic fluid), Impression full width; 12 sp grey labels over 16 sp values. No pills, no highlighted EFW, no inner box.
  • Photo buttons stay outlined 48 dp buttons at the bottom of the body: they are actions.
  • Rhythm: teal bands make the Scans tab recognisable next to the blue Visits tab.
