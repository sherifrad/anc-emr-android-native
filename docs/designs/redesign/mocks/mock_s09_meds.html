Mock S09: Meds tab (restyle only)

For slice 9, revised after the owner's feedback ("more elaborating than needed with all these squares"). Restyle only: no Meds stage 2. Mona Ragab's sample has no medications, so her tab shows the empty state. The list uses the in-repo demo fixture DemoFixtures (Iron Polymaltose Complex 100 mg, Oral, Once daily, Active; Calcium Carbonate + Vitamin D3 600 mg / 400 IU, Oral, Once daily with meals, Active; Folic Acid 5 mg, Completed), labelled as fixtures.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: today's prescriptions, read at a glance and changed with few taps, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

  • One medication, one row. First line: name in bold and the dose. Second line: route and frequency in grey. At the end: the status as a plain word ("Active" grey; "Patient non-compliant" red, the one coloured word). Rows split by a hairline. No card per drug, no box per field, no fill.
  • Stop stays one tap: the red word "Stop" sits under the status on every active row (48 dp wide, about 40 dp tall within the 48 dp row; labelled "Stop <drug>").
  • Edit and delete move into the opened row. Tapping the row opens its start date, notes and side effects as plain grey-label text, with Edit and Delete at the end. Fewer controls per row means less to read on every visit.
  • Quick entry stays compact: "Add medication" is one 48 dp row that opens the search field, Common / Recent and the drug tiles (plain outlined 48 dp tiles with name and dose); the undo bar is one 48 dp line.
  • Titles in sentence case ("Active prescriptions (2)"), Prescribe without the "+".
