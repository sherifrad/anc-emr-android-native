Mock S12: Registration flow and patient editor

Covers RegistrationStepHeader, RegistrationStepFooter, RegistrationStepText, PatientEditor (Identity and Obstetric steps plus standalone), and PatientStatusEditor. Real sample only: Mona Ragab / منى رجب, G4 P1 A2, 27+2 wks, EDD 11/02/2027, plus bracketed placeholders where a value is not in the sample.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: clinical registration forms where every control is obvious at arm's length and mixed English/Arabic entry behaves predictably. Antislop dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 (pressed and focus states only, no new animation).

  • Fix under-12 sp text in the step header: today's segment glyph (labelSmall, 11 sp), status word (11 sp), and "Current" label (11 sp) all break the 12 sp floor. Raise all three to RecordType.Tag (12 sp), keep the 1.3x label suppression required by RegistrationFlowUiTest, place the header on surface with a full-contrast divider, and use RecordShapes.Small (10 dp) on step bars instead of 3 dp.
  • Stop floating-label clipping on narrow 360 dp viewports: move fields in slice 12 to FormFieldLabel (12 sp SemiBold in FieldPatient indigo or FieldObstetric sage) above 10 dp OutlinedTextField boxes, so long labels like "Clinical attention and high-risk alerts (one alert per line)" never truncate in a border notch at 1.3x scale.
  • Arabic name input from the right edge: reuse isRtlText from PatientNameText.kt on the Full name input so typing منى رجب sets TextDirection.Rtl and TextAlign.Right inside the box while the English field label stays anchored at start.
  • Replace the misleading Lock icon on unencrypted photos: today PatientEditor puts a locked padlock icon next to "Photos are stored on this phone (not encrypted separately)" at 11 sp. Keep the honest notice at 14 sp RecordType.Secondary and remove the Lock icon.
  • Buttons aligned with slice 11: RegistrationStepFooter keeps FlowRow (required so 2.0x text wraps cleanly) with RecordShapes.Small (10 dp) buttons and RecordType.Action (14 sp); standalone PatientEditor and PatientStatusEditor switch from uneven pill EditorActions to equal-width 48 dp SheetCancelSaveRow.

6. Compose mapping

  1. RegistrationStepHeader: wrapped in Surface(color = colorScheme.surface) with bottom HorizontalDivider(color = outlineVariant); step headline uses RecordType.SectionTitle and pregnancy counter uses RecordType.Secondary; segment bars use RecordShapes.Small; glyph, status word, and "Current" all move to RecordType.Tag (12 sp), preserving WORDS_MAX_FONT_SCALE = 1.3f and registrationSegment-${index + 1} semantics.
  2. RegistrationStepFooter: full-contrast HorizontalDivider(outlineVariant), FlowRow(spacedBy(8.dp)) keeping minimumTouchTarget(); Back, Skip, and Next/Finish all use shape = RecordShapes.Small (10 dp) and RecordType.Action (14 sp).
  3. PatientEditor (Identity & Obstetric groups): replaces private EditorSectionCard with RecordSectionCard(title, FieldFamilies.patient()) and RecordSectionCard(title, FieldFamilies.obstetric()); uses FormFieldLabel + RecordShapes.Small inputs; keeps patientEditorGroups and all existing testTags (including patientAgeText).
  4. Arabic name input rule: patientName checks isRtlText(draft.values.displayName) (reusing existing ui/components/PatientNameText.kt helper) and applies TextDirection.Rtl and TextAlign.Right to the input textStyle when Arabic characters lead, while the field label stays at start.
  5. PatientStatusEditor and standalone PatientEditor: EditorPageHeader adopts RecordType.HeaderName (22 sp) + RecordType.Secondary (14 sp); PatientStatusEditor wraps reason input in RecordSectionCard(FieldFamilies.patient()) with StatusTag for target status; bottom actions switch to SheetCancelSaveRow (saveTag = "savePatient" / "savePatientStatus"); GenericEditorErrorCard moves to errorContainer / onErrorContainer.

7. Decisions for the owner (this slice)

  1. Arabic name input direction: Apply isRtlText to patientName so منى رجب starts from the right edge inside the field while English names start from the left. Recommend: accept right-edge start.
  2. Step header font size floor: Raise segment glyph, status word, and "Current" from 11 sp to 12 sp (RecordType.Tag) while keeping the 1.3x hide threshold required by RegistrationFlowUiTest. Recommend: accept 12 sp with 1.3x cap.
  3. Unencrypted photo notice: Remove the Lock icon next to "Photos are stored on this phone (not encrypted separately)" and render it as 14 sp RecordType.Secondary text. Recommend: keep the honest notice without the padlock.
