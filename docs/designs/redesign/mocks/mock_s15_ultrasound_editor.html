Design reasoning

Reading this as: a mid-clinic ultrasound charting screen and photo viewer used between live probe views, where one-tap normal defaults, clean label-above-field spacing, and 48 dp targets reduce scan-to-save time. Antislop dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 (pressed and focus states only, no new animation).

  • Obstetric family (teal) throughout: match ScansTab, DatingEditor, and PreviousPregnancyEditor using RecordSectionCard(FieldFamilies.obstetric()) (#1D5149 light / #9FD6CC dark labels), so every ultrasound form looks unmistakably part of the scan domain without rainbow pills or blue-purple drift.
  • Make the live summary + 1-tap shortcut distinct: restyle Card 0 (ultrasoundLiveSummary) in calm FieldObstetricBg (#E3F0EE / #1D3836) with 16 sp live sentence, 48 dp RecordShapes.Small Fill normal scan on white surface, and a quiet TextButton for Clear findings so Clear is never tapped by accident.
  • Fix 360 dp notch clipping & sub-12 sp text: move scanDate, gaWeeks, gaDays, and all 6 biometry fields to external 12 sp FormFieldLabels over 10 dp boxes (so "Estimated fetal weight (EFW)" and "GA weeks (0–45)" never truncate in half-width notches); raise 11 sp labels (BPD, HC, AC, FL, FHR & EFW, Photo 1, 1x zoom reset, FactBadges) to 12–16 sp; drop the 11 sp Lock footer.
  • Guided steps & GA convention: keep the 4-item accordion (fetalNumber, presentation, placenta, liquor) with 10 dp 48 dp-target RecordFilterChips when open and clean SummaryRows (no downward ▾ chevron) when closed; format scanCalculatedGaCard and applyScanGa as 27+2 wks (Owner Decision 9) and move redatingHint to semantic amber.
  • Fix photo attach & viewer ergonomics: stack Take photo and Choose from gallery full-width 48 dp buttons (zero 360 dp overflow at 1.3x/2.0x scale), give ZoomableImage's 1x reset button 10 dp corners and 14 sp text on solid surface, and restyle UltrasoundPhotoViewerDialog without its decorative camera circle, 11 sp pills, or 8 dp clickable dots.
