Real sample data only: Mona Ragab / منى رجب, 27+2 wks, G4 P1 A2, 110/70, A+, EDD 11/02/2027 (note: Hb 11.2 belongs to labs, not visit entry). Bracketed placeholders mark values not in Mona's sample. Fundal height entry does not exist in VisitEditor.kt today (removed in commit 180da0d; legacy values round-trip silently).

Design reasoning

Reading this as: a bedside antenatal charting flow completed dozens of times a clinic session, where fewer taps, clear numeric targets, and calm hierarchy reduce eye and thumb fatigue. Antislop dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 (pressed and focus states only, no new animation).

  • Replace 5 stacked header blocks with one calm Patient family banner + labelled step bar: today the top repeats Mona's name twice, shows 5 sub-12 sp rainbow pills (with ObstetricParityBadge wrongly dropping A2), and draws 4 anonymous 4 dp lines. Slice 14 replaces that with one compact FieldPatientBg strip (PatientNameText, risk StatusTag only if Moderate/High, and BandChips for 27+2 wks, G4 P1 A2, A+) plus a 4-tab VisitStepHeader (Date & BP, Wt & FHR, Findings, Review) so doctors can jump to any step in one tap.
  • Clear BP and 52 dp numeric steppers: keep all three BP tags (visitSystolic, visitDiastolic, visitBloodPressure) with 12 sp FormFieldLabels and 10 dp boxes; upgrade highBpWarning from rose secondaryContainer to semantic amber/red; give ValueStepper crisp 52 dp outlined 10 dp buttons and a fluid center box; raise the FHR baseline badge from 11 sp translucent teal with emoji to quiet StatusWord (12 sp).
  • Cut Step 2 scroll by ~750 dp: replace 10 stacked full-width BigChoiceButtons and noisy (Flag) suffixes with one "Bedside findings" card of 48 dp RecordFilterChip rows (Oedema, Fetal movements, Urine protein) and show StatusWord("Flagged", ...) beside the label only when an abnormal choice is picked.
