Mock S13: History and dating editors

Covers OtherHistoriesEditor, PreviousPregnancyEditor (level 3), DatingEditor (level 3), and DatingFieldVisibility. Sample: Mona Ragab, G4 P1 A2, 27+2 wks, EDD 11/02/2027 (2027-02-11 in ISO date inputs), with bracketed placeholders where a value is not in the sample.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: clinical data-entry editors where conditional fields, dating dates, and pregnancy outcome codes must read as plain obstetric terms. Antislop dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 (pressed and focus states only, no new animation).

  • Replace 11 sp labels and 11 sp vault footers: QuickPresetChips titles and embryo_age currently use labelMedium (11 sp), and all three editors append repetitive 11 sp AES-256 / Tamper-Evident Lock footers. Upgrade active field labels to FormFieldLabel (12 sp SemiBold in FieldBackground or FieldObstetric) and drop the decorative 11 sp vault footers and hardcoded numbered card titles ("1. Baseline Dating Parameters", "2. ...").
  • Map raw outcome enum codes to real clinical labels in PreviousPregnancyEditor: today the chips literally read LiveBirth, Stillbirth, EarlyPregnancyLoss, TerminationOfPregnancy, and CS. Display existing string resources ("Live birth", "Stillbirth / IUFD", "Early pregnancy loss", "Termination of pregnancy", "Cesarean") via RecordFilterChip (10 dp, 48 dp target) while keeping the stored codes untouched in state and SQLite.
  • Eliminate box-inside-a-box in DatingEditor and fix GA/EDD format: today Card 2 wraps a 12 dp primaryContainer card inside another elevated card, prints raw ISO 2027-02-11 and 27w 2d, and leaves an empty Card 2 header when blank. Show Card 2 only when calculated values exist, and render two FieldObstetricBg tiles: 11/02/2027 (formatDmy) and 27+2 wks (shortWeeksText).
  • Enforce 48 dp targets on every chip and action: add .minimumTouchTarget() to fillAllNegativeHistories, all preset and choice chips (RecordFilterChip wraps a 48 dp hit target), embryoDay3 / embryoDay5 / embryoDay6, and date calendar buttons.
