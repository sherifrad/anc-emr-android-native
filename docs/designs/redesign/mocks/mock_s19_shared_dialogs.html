Mock S19: Shell and shared dialogs

Covers ui/DeleteUndoUi.kt (DeleteConfirmDialog, UndoBarHost), ui/AncApp.kt dialogs only (showNewPatientInfo, showDiscardDialog, draftRecoveryPrompt), ui/AncDatePicker.kt (AncDatePickerDialog, ClinicalDateField), and ui/InAppCameraDialog.kt. All strings come from strings.xml and DemoFixtures (Mona Ragab, Iron Polymaltose Complex, 2026-05-07, 2026-11-16).

Design reasoning

Reading this as: shared clinical interruption chrome where clinicians confirm deletions, recover unsaved drafts after lock or crash, pick calendar dates without hand-typing errors, and capture scan photos in dark rooms without missing a control. Antislop dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 (pressed and focus states only, no new animation).

  • Error-colored destructive confirms across all dialogs: DeleteConfirmDialog (Delete), AncApp showDiscardDialog (reusing DiscardChangesDialog), and draftRecoveryPrompt (Discard and start fresh) stop dressing irreversible actions in primary navy pills, while safe actions (Cancel, Keep editing, Continue) hold primary weight.
  • Calm docked UndoBarHost with zero elevation: replace the 12 dp shadowed grey undo slab with a 10 dp FieldPatientBg bar outlined in outline (3.91:1 / 4.55:1) and 14 sp wrapping copy; frame DeleteMessageBar in errorContainer with a 1 dp error border and high-contrast onErrorContainer Close action.
  • Picker-only date chrome (Owner rule 2026-11-16): restyle AncDatePickerDialog on 16 dp surface (replacing 28 dp warm grey), remove the M3 keyboard toggle icon (showModeToggle = false), and make ClinicalDateField readOnly = true with full-field tap to open the picker (keeping EditableText semantics for tests).
  • Readable InAppCameraDialog on pure black: replace the 1.24:1 light-mode dark-navy Use photo button with high-contrast white fill and navy ink (21:1 boundary, 16.92:1 text), move Close camera to the top bar away from thumb capture/confirm taps, and place permission-denied and capture errors inside bordered dark cards.

9. Decisions for the owner (max 5)

  1. Enforce picker-only ClinicalDateField (readOnly = true + showModeToggle = false): Audit found all 11 ClinicalDateField callers still allow manual keyboard entry, and M3 DatePicker exposes a pencil text-input switch. Recommend: set readOnly = true (tap box or icon opens picker) and showModeToggle = false; update the 3 performTextInput calls in MedicationFormTest in the test lane.
  2. Migrate DeliveryPlanSheet deliveryOutcomeDateField to picker-only ClinicalDateField + Clear: Audit found deliveryOutcomeDateField is the only date input in the app with no picker at all (raw typed OutlinedTextField). Recommend: migrate it to ClinicalDateField(..., allowClear = true) (either in Slice 19 or an immediate Slice 11 follow-up) and update DeliveryPlanSheetTest in the test lane.
  3. Historical PreviousPregnancyEditor date (pregnancyDateText): Stored data allows partial/year entries (e.g. "2022"). Recommend: keep picker-only ClinicalDateField(showTodayButton = false, allowClear = true) for uniform rule compliance unless historical year-only typing must remain an explicit exception.
  4. Error-colored destructive buttons and stale draft callout: Recommend: reuse DiscardChangesDialog in AncApp.kt, color Delete and Discard and start fresh in error, and box draft_recovery_stale in StatusModerate amber.
  5. InAppCameraDialog contrast and Close camera position: Recommend: fix the 1.24:1 light-mode Use photo contrast defect with white fill / navy text on black and move Close camera to top-end (or inside the permission card) with 16.dp frames.
