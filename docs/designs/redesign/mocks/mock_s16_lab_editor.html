Mock S16: Lab editor and lab batch

Covers ui/LabEditor.kt, ui/LabBatchScreen.kt, and display-only styling in ui/ClinicalPresets.kt (level 3) plus backward-compatible helpers in ui/DesignSystem.kt. Real sample data only: Mona Ragab / منى رجب, 27+2 wks, G4 P1 A2, 110/70, A+, Hb 11.2, EDD 11/02/2027 (2027-02-11 in ISO date fields), paired with real catalog panels and tests from ClinicalCatalogRepository. Values absent from Mona's sample are shown in brackets.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: high-frequency clinical laboratory charting where clinicians enter single tests or multi-test panels from paper lab slips and need calm hierarchy, large tap targets, and minimal scrolling. Antislop dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 (pressed and focus states only, no new animation).

  • Background family (sand) across all lab entry: match LabsTab (LabReportItem.kt) using FieldFamilies.background() (#F1EDE4 / #594A2C light, #36301F / #E0CFA6 dark), eliminating ActionRose accents, ALL-CAPS red-striped headers, and 11 sp rainbow pills.
  • Single unified patient banner and metadata bar: replace the duplicated patient name, old ClinicalPatientCard(EDITOR_SUMMARY) (which dropped A2 and kept Normal Risk), and two duplicate BatchInfoBars with one shared LabPatientBanner and unified BatchInfoBar showing 27+2 wks (Owner Decision 9) and a clean Change action.
  • Faster data entry with less vertical scroll on 360 dp: in LabEditor, shrink the oversized 36 sp pink readout box into a calm sand readout strip with BandChip, convert 5 stacked 56 dp dipstick buttons into one compact RecordFilterChip row, collapse the 28-test catalog preset list to 5 items with Show all 28, and move the squeezed 3-button bottom row to MedicationSheet's 2-row footer so labels never clip.
  • Un-crush 360 dp batch panels and hit 48 dp everywhere: stack Choose and + Add N into a 50/50 48 dp row below full-width panel titles, wrap Often added chips in FlowRow, raise labBatchDeleteItem from 32 dp to 48 dp, style Enter and Review with RecordSectionCard and StatusWord, and bring 1-tap lab presets + ClinicalDateField into the batch metadata dialog.

Removes duplicate patient names, fixes missing A2 in parity, removes sub-12 sp rainbow pills, and aligns GA to 27+2 wks across both editors.
  2. Calm readout strip, RecordFilterChip dipstick row, and collapsed test presets in LabEditor: Recommend accept. Replaces the 36 sp pink box and 5 stacked 56 dp dipstick buttons with a compact sand strip and 1-row chips, and collapses the 28-item preset list to 5 + Show all 28 (display-only TextButton styling in level-3 ClinicalPresets.kt).
  3. 2-row MedicationSheet bottom bar in LabEditor: Recommend accept. Uses SheetCancelSaveRow (Cancel / Save) plus full-width Save and add another (or Save and next test) below so button labels never clip on 360 dp at 2.0x scale.
  4. Stacked 48 dp panel actions and upgraded metadata dialog in LabBatchScreen: Recommend accept. Gives long panel names full width above 50/50 Choose / + Add N buttons, wraps Often added in FlowRow, and adds ClinicalDateField + labNamePresets to Batch details while keeping exact labBatchDate and labBatchLabName tags.
  5. Restyle Enter and Review to Sand/Primary tokens, raise delete to 48 dp, drop Lock footer, and surface LabBatchDraft.errors: Recommend accept. Replaces ActionRose, 32 dp remove icon, 11 sp pills, and triangle arrows with RecordSectionCard, StatusWord, and GenericEditorErrorCard (preserving CBC · 6 elements for LabBatchUiTest).
