For slice 11: the medication sheet, the delivery plan sheet and their discard dialog. Chrome only: the sheet surface, header, field and group labels, the button row and the dialog. Fields, their order inside the medication form, chips, the status buttons, the save path and every tag stay. Patient: Mona Ragab (27+2 wks, G4 P1 A2, EDD 11/02/2027). The filled medication is the in-repo fixture (DemoFixtures: Iron Polymaltose Complex, 100 mg, Oral, Once daily, started 2026-04-04, "Routine antenatal iron prophylaxis; well tolerated"). The filled delivery plan is Mothercare, 12000. The delivered frame is layout only (Mona has not delivered), with empty fields.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: a short form opened many times a day, where a wrong tap loses typing. Dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

  • The sheet matches the cards it edits. White surface (today a warm grey, surfaceContainerLow), 16 dp top corners (today 28), navy 18 sp bold title like every section title.
  • Labels in the family colour of the card. Group and field labels become 12 sp SemiBold in the colour the same field wears on its card: sand for medications (Background family), plum for delivery. Today they are 14 to 15 sp in three different weights.
  • One label per field everywhere. The delivery sheet shows the card's sentence-case labels ("Agreed delivery hospital"). The title-case strings ("Agreed Delivery Hospital") stay in strings.xml because they are the keys written into the notes.
  • Buttons: radius 10 (today full pills), 48 dp, 14 sp. A hairline above the button row marks where the scrolling form ends. The delivery sheet's row gets the same 16 dp side padding as the medication sheet (today it runs edge to edge).
  • Dropped: the lock icon beside a read-only route (the label already says read-only), the "No record" placeholder inside empty delivery boxes, the "&" in "Save & add another".
  • Discard dialog: white, radius 16, 16 sp body (today 13 sp), "Discard" in the error colour so the destructive word does not look like the safe one.
  • Not in this slice: the M3 chips (radius 8, rose when selected) and the Active / Stopped buttons (radius 12, check icon) are shared with every editor and change in the editor slices.

6. Compose mapping

  1. Both sheets: ModalBottomSheet(containerColor = colorScheme.surface, shape = RecordShapes.Sheet) where RecordShapes.Sheet = RoundedCornerShape(topStart = 16.dp, topEnd = 16.dp) (new token). Content wrapped in CompositionLocalProvider(LocalFieldFamily provides FieldFamilies.background()) (medication) or .delivery().
  2. Title: Text(title, RecordType.SectionTitle, primary, Modifier.padding(start = 16.dp, end = 16.dp, bottom = 8.dp).semantics { heading() }).
  3. Labels: FormFieldLabel(text) (added in slice 10, ui/components/FormFieldLabel.kt) = Text(text, RecordType.FieldLabel, LocalFieldFamily.current?.label ?: onSurfaceVariant).
  4. Footer: HorizontalDivider(outlineVariant), then SheetCancelSaveRow(..., modifier = Modifier.padding(horizontal = 16.dp, vertical = 12.dp)); buttons shape = RecordShapes.Small, heightIn(min = 48.dp), label RecordType.Action.
  5. Discard: AlertDialog(shape = RecordShapes.Large, containerColor = surface, title RecordType.SectionTitle onSurface, text RecordType.FieldValue onSurfaceVariant); Discard TextButton text in colorScheme.error.
