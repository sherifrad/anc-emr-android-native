Mock S05: Patient list chrome and the actions sheet

For slice 5. The list holds one real patient, Mona Ragab, shown as Moderate risk so the counts are honest for that list: All 1, High risk 0, Moderate risk 1, Low risk 0, Delivered 0, Archived 0. Her case ID and age are not in the sample, so the sheet shows "[case ID]". Cards are the slice 1 card, unchanged.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: the clinic's home screen, scanned many times a day, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

  • One chip style. Today every filter selects in its own colour (navy, red, pink, teal). After: every chip is outlined, the selected one is filled navy with white text. Risk is a word in the chip; colour does not carry it.
  • Words match the cards. The card says "Moderate risk"; the filter said "Medium Risk". The filter reads "Moderate risk", and all chips use sentence case (string values, owner decision 7).
  • Two radii. Search pill (24 dp), count pill (12 dp), sheet rows (12 dp) and chips (8 dp) become 10 dp; cards and the sheet top stay 16 dp.
  • Count as text. "1 Patients" in a tinted pill becomes plain 14 sp text under the title (the plural bug is listed as owner decision 8; the string stays as is in this slice).
  • No decoration. The "+" before "New patient", the "›" at the end of each sheet row and the icons before sheet actions repeat the words, so they go. The search icon stays: it marks the field.
  • Sheet: rows 56 dp, neutral, the primary action (Record visit) in the patient tint, Archive keeps red text plus its subtitle (word plus colour). The header line "Case: X · " drops the trailing dot when age is missing.
  • Empty states keep their sentences, now from string resources, in an outlined 16 dp card.
