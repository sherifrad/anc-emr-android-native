The second row uses the in-repo demo fixture DemoFixtures visit ...0106 (2026-07-25, BP 120/78, 71.5 kg, fundal height 28 cm), labelled as a fixture; its GA (28+0 wks) follows from that fixture's EDD 2026-10-17. Flagged states use bracketed placeholders.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: a timeline of bedside checks a doctor scans for the one abnormal number, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

  • A flat list, not cards. Each visit is a row on the white tab surface: the date as a 16 sp bold heading with Delete and Edit at its end, one grey line under it ("28+0 wks, scan linked"), then the vitals as plain label-over-value text. Visits are separated by a hairline. No card, no box inside a box, no tinted square per field.
  • Vitals in two plain columns: Blood pressure, Weight, Fetal heart rate, Fundal height (12 sp grey label, 16 sp value).
  • Flags as coloured words, not pills: "Raised, recheck" (amber word), "Severe, urgent", "Bradycardia (<110)", "Small for dates" (red words), "Large for dates" (amber). Normal values carry no word. The "▲", "✓", "⚠️" and "·" go; owner decision 6 also cleans the bp_alert_* strings.
  • Pre-eclampsia is a red label "Pre-eclampsia risk" over the existing sentence, at the top of the row, without a filled box.
  • GA as "28+0 wks" (owner decision 9), in the grey line under the date.
  • Dropped: the tinted card per visit, the bordered grid box inside it, the column dividers, the 11 sp labels, the green "Normal ✓" pills, the duplicate BP alert in the visit header, the "+" on Record visit.

4. Compose mapping

  1. Tab: LazyColumn on colorScheme.surface; header item with RecordType.SectionTitle and the Record visit button (48 dp, word only); HorizontalDivider(color = outlineVariant) between visit items.
  2. Visit item: shared RecordListItem(title = visit.date, subtitle = "28+0 wks, scan linked", actions = { DeleteIconButton; SectionTextAction(Edit) }) (new, slice 6): heading row min 48 dp, title RecordType.CardName, subtitle RecordType.Secondary.
  3. Vitals: shared PlainField(label, value, flag) (new in MetricCell.kt): 12 sp label, 16 sp value that wraps, optional StatusWord; two per row with weight(1f).
  4. Flags: shared StatusWord(text, tone) (new in StatusTag.kt): plain Text(RecordType.Tag.copy(fontWeight = Bold)) in error, amber (StatusModerateText / dark) or onSurfaceVariant; no fill, no border.
  5. Pre-eclampsia: StatusWord(High, "Pre-eclampsia risk") over Text(RecordType.FieldValue), tag preEclampsiaWarning-${id} on the column.
