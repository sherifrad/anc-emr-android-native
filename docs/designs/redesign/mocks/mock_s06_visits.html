Mock S06: Visits tab (band cards)

Third version, in the middle style the owner chose from the meds options (Option A): one card per visit, a blue band holding the chips and the date, a quiet white body with plain label-over-value text, one-tap actions. Mock D's tinted box per field was "too many squares"; the flat list was "too plain". Owner sample: Mona Ragab has BP 110/70 (date and other vitals not in the sample, shown as "[visit date]" and "Not recorded"). The second card uses the in-repo demo fixture DemoFixtures visit ...0106 (2026-07-25, 120/78, 71.5 kg, fundal height 28 cm), labelled; its GA 28+0 wks follows from the fixture's EDD 2026-10-17. Flagged states use bracketed placeholders.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: a timeline of bedside checks a doctor scans for the one abnormal number, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

  • One container per visit. The card's own top band (Patient family blue, #E9EEF8) holds the chips and the date with Delete and Edit; the white body holds the vitals. A band is part of the card, not a box inside it.
  • Chips on the band: the GA ("28+0 wks", white chip with blue text), "Scan linked" when linked, and the visit's worst flag as a filled word chip: "Severe BP" or "Pre-eclampsia risk" (red), "Raised BP" (amber). A normal visit carries no flag chip, so flagged visits stand out down the list.
  • Quiet body: Blood pressure, Weight, Fetal heart rate, Fundal height as 12 sp grey label over 16 sp value in two columns, with the per-value flag as a coloured word under the value ("Raised, recheck", "Bradycardia (<110)", "Large for dates"). No tinted box per field, no pills, no ▲ ✓ ⚠.
  • Pre-eclampsia sentence sits at the top of the body as a red label over the existing sentence.
  • Rhythm and identity: every visit card starts with the same blue band, so the list reads as a timeline; scans use teal bands, labs sand, so each tab has its own colour.
