Mock S08: Labs tab (band cards)

Third version, in the owner's chosen middle style (Option A): one card per report, a sand band with the status chip, the lab line and the date, a quiet body with the tests. Mona Ragab's sample has no investigations, so her tab is empty. Populated reports use the in-repo demo fixture DemoFixtures (2026-07-25: "Complete Blood Count" 11.6 g/dL, ref 11.0 - 15.0, Al Borg Medical Labs; "Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (75g) - 2h Post-Load" 112 mg/dL, ref < 140, Al Mokhtabar Labs). The CBC panel card uses test names from LabReportGroupingOwnerFixTest with bracketed results. The grouping rule (a panel needs at least two members, otherwise the lone test is under Individual investigations) is untouched.

Design reasoning

Reading this as: a report list where one abnormal result must jump out, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

  • One container per report with a sand band (Background family, #F1EDE4): a filled status chip ("All normal" white chip; "1 abnormal" red chip), the date as title with "Add test", and a grey line "Al Borg Medical Labs, 1 test, 28+0 wks".
  • Quiet body: tests as rows split by hairlines: name 16 sp, grey "Hematology, ref 11.0 - 15.0", the result 16 sp bold at the end, delete after it. An abnormal result is red with the word "Abnormal".
  • Panels are sub-headings inside the body ("Complete Blood Count (CBC) (3)", 48 dp, opens and closes, its own status word and a chevron), never a box inside the card.
  • Dropped: the "N ABNORMAL ▲" and "ALL NORMAL ✓" capsules, the bordered panel boxes, the uppercase "INDIVIDUAL INVESTIGATIONS", the duplicate footer "+ Add Another Test to this Report" (same handler as the band's "Add test").
