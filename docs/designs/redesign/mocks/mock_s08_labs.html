Design reasoning

Reading this as: a report list where one abnormal result must jump out, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

  • Flat rows. A report is a heading (date, with "Add test" at its end), a grey line ("Al Borg Medical Labs, 1 test, 28+0 wks") and its status word, then one row per test split by hairlines. Reports are split by a hairline too. No card, no nested panel box.
  • Panels are sub-headings, not boxes. "Complete Blood Count (CBC) (3)" is a 48 dp row that opens and closes its tests, with its status word and a chevron (it shows the open state). "Individual investigations" is the same kind of sub-heading, in sentence case.
  • Status as words, no fills: "All normal" in grey, "1 abnormal" in red; each abnormal result is red with the word "Abnormal" under it. The "N ABNORMAL ▲" and "ALL NORMAL ✓" capsules go.
  • Rows: test name 16 sp, grey "Hematology, ref 11.0 - 15.0", the result 16 sp bold at the end, delete after it.
  • One add per report: the footer "+ Add Another Test to this Report" repeated the header action with the same handler; it goes.
