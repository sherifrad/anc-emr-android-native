### 3. Decisions for the owner to confirm
1. **Enforce `readOnly = true` on `ClinicalDateField` and `showModeToggle = false` on `AncDatePickerDialog`**:
   Stops manual typing across all 11 existing `ClinicalDateField` callers and inside the M3 calendar dialog while keeping `SemanticsProperties.EditableText` for [AncWorkflowTest.kt](file:///Users/sherifgamalradwan/anc-emr-android-native/app/src/androidTest/java/com/sherifradwan/ancnative/AncWorkflowTest.kt#L270). **Recommend**: approve, and update the 3 `performTextInput` assertions on `medDate` in [MedicationFormTest.kt](file:///Users/sherifgamalradwan/anc-emr-android-native/app/src/androidTest/java/com/sherifradwan/ancnative/MedicationFormTest.kt#L235-L243) in the test lane.
2. **Migrate `DeliveryPlanSheet` `deliveryOutcomeDateField` to `ClinicalDateField` + Clear chip**:
   `deliveryOutcomeDateField` is the only date input without a picker, and its hint permits an empty unknown date. **Recommend**: add `allowClear: Boolean = false` (`${tag}ClearButton`) on `ClinicalDateField`, replace the raw `OutlinedTextField` in [DeliveryPlanSheet.kt](file:///Users/sherifgamalradwan/anc-emr-android-native/app/src/main/java/com/sherifradwan/ancnative/ui/patienthub/components/DeliveryPlanSheet.kt#L248) with `ClinicalDateField(..., allowClear = true, showTodayButton = true, externalLabel = true, useRecordStyle = true)` (in Slice 19 or a Slice 11 follow-up), and update [DeliveryPlanSheetTest.kt](file:///Users/sherifgamalradwan/anc-emr-android-native/app/src/androidTest/java/com/sherifradwan/ancnative/DeliveryPlanSheetTest.kt#L300-L353) in the test lane.
3. **Historical `PreviousPregnancyEditor` `pregnancyDateText`**:
   `PastDeliveryRowModel` tolerates historical year-only values (e.g. `"2022"`), whereas a picker-only `ClinicalDateField` writes full `YYYY-MM-DD` dates via the picker's year/month/day grid. **Recommend**: keep `pregnancyDateText` picker-only (`ClinicalDateField(showTodayButton = false, allowClear = true)`) for strict rule uniformity unless historical year-only typing is retained as an exception.
4. **Error-colored destructive actions and stale draft callout**:
   **Recommend**: reuse `DiscardChangesDialog` directly in [AncApp.kt](file:///Users/sherifgamalradwan/anc-emr-android-native/app/src/main/java/com/sherifradwan/ancnative/ui/AncApp.kt#L308-L332), style `Delete` in `DeleteConfirmDialog` and `Discard and start fresh` in `draftRecoveryPrompt` in `error`, and place `draft_recovery_stale` in a `StatusModerate` amber callout.
5. **`InAppCameraDialog` contrast and `Close camera` placement**:
   **Recommend**: switch preview/review frames to `RecordShapes.Large` (`16 dp`), fix the `1.24:1` `Use photo` contrast defect with white fill / navy text on black, move `Close camera` to the top-end bar in live/review states, and frame permission-denied and capture errors in dark cards.
