**Recommendation:** Restore agreed EDD as always editable (as before), or prove `showAgreedEdd` is `true` for every reachable dating method/state and cannot hide an existing/non-empty `agreedEdd` value; otherwise keep it visible (at least when `draft.values.agreedEdd` is non-empty or when adjudication is clinically applicable).
