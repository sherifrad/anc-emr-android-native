### Round 3m Independent Review

**Findings:**
1. **Hard Blocker** — `SaveVisitCommand.kt:49,54`:
   `parseBloodPressure(bloodPressure)?.let(::isPlausibleBloodPressure) != true` and `parseWeightKg(weight)?.let(::isPlausibleWeightKg) != true` use method references rather than direct invocations. This breaks gate `STATIC-r3m` (`check_r3m.py:116-120` checks for literal `isPlausibleBloodPressure(` and `isPlausibleWeightKg(`), causing S68 to fail. Change to `val bp = parseBloodPressure(bloodPressure); if (bp == null || !isPlausibleBloodPressure(bp))` and analogous for weight.
2. **Should-fix** — `VisitVitals.kt:8` & `VisitVitalsTest.kt:64`:
   `weightRegex` defines `(?:(?:\.(\d))?(?:\s*(?:kg|kilograms|kilogram))?|,(\d))$`. The unit group is only inside the period branch; trailing unit after a comma decimal is rejected. `"68,5 kg"` returns `null` despite Q84 requiring comma and optional unit support. `VisitVitalsTest.kt:64` (`assertNull(parseWeightKg("68,5 kg"))`) codifies this bug. Regex should permit the unit after `,(\d)`.
3. **Nit** — `ClinicalBadges.kt:166-167`:
   `val res = bpAlertResource(raw) ?: return` followed by `val isSevere = bpLevel(raw) == BpLevel.SEVERE` parses and evaluates `bpLevel` twice on every recomposition.
