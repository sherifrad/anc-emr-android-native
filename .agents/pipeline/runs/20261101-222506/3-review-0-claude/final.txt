**Should-fix**
1. **Open-on-entry rules are not re-applied after "Add another test".**
   - The open flags are only set once, in `rememberSaveable { mutableStateOf(initialEditDetails) }` (lines 144–149). Line 595 and line 633 then set `editDetailsOpen = step.item.unit.isBlank() && ...`.
   - After a loaded test is saved with "Add another", the new draft is blank. `editDetailsOpen` can still be false, so `labName` is hidden under the title "New test".
   - `addTestsOpen`, `flagSectionOpen` and `noteSectionOpen` also keep the previous test's state.
   - This only matters if the same composable instance survives across drafts, which the panel-queue design assumes. Re-derive the flags when `draft.labId` changes, inside the existing `LaunchedEffect(draft.labId)` at line 175.
2. **Possible vertical text at 160% font in the flag/note chip row (lines 471–493).**
   - The Row has no `weight` and no `maxLines`. "Flag · abnormal" plus "Note ✓" can overflow or wrap inside a chip. A `FlowRow` or `maxLines = 1` would fix it.
3. **Same risk in the banner at lines 648–657.**
   - The text `"New test: add it to your lab test list?"` has no `weight(1f)`, so the "Add to list" button can be squeezed into a vertical stack. Add `Modifier.weight(1f)` to the text.
4. **Strings are hard-coded in `LabEditor.kt`, against L309 ("each once").**
   - The affected text is `"Test $currentPanelIndex of $panelTotalCount · …"` (333), the preset labels `"Negative"`, `"Non-reactive"`, `"Normal"` and `"Positive"` (459), and `"Note ✓"` (488).
   - Some of the other hard-coded strings may predate this round. I could not diff to tell. Examples are `"1. Laboratory Details & Date"`, `"📦 Diagnostic Panels:"` and `"Add to list"`.
