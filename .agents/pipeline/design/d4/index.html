Entry screen mock-ups

Approved designs only, sized for a small phone (about 360 dp). Use A− and A+ for text size (limited to 90–130%) and the switch for light or dark. Light is the default. Nothing is built in the app yet beyond laboratory (new) and ultrasound.

Part 1Laboratory, ultrasound, medication. Part 2Obstetric history, previous pregnancies, dating, clinical attention entry, delivery plan. Part 3Patient directory, patient chart, visit and vitals entry.

Every list of predefined choices ends with “+ Add other”: the value is used at once and saved for later patients.
