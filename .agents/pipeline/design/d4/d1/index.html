1 · Laboratory entry

Approved design

  • Faster: the keypad stays on screen; qualitative results move on by themselves after one tap; a panel adds all its tests.
  • Keeps: test, result, unit, reference range, flag (auto, can be overridden), note, lab name, date.
  • Try: Add tab, then a panel; type 16 for haemoglobin; + Add other under a result list.

2 · Ultrasound entry

Approved design

  • Faster: “Fill normal scan” answers the usual findings; after each answer the next finding opens; the report line updates live.
  • Keeps: date, GA, number, presentation, placenta, liquor, FHR, BPD, HC, AC, FL, EFW, impression, photos, link to visit.
  • Try: raise GA weeks four times to see the dating hint; tap “From my choices”.

3 · Medication entry

Approved design

  • Faster: one tap on a drug adds it with its usual dose and today’s date; chips on the new line change anything, with Undo.
  • Keeps: name, dose, route, frequency, start, end, status, prophylaxis, non-compliance, side effects, notes.
  • Try: add Low-Dose Aspirin, stop Labetalol, then press Undo.
