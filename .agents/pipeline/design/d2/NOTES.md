## 1. Overview & Architectural Principles

These mock-ups address Dr. Sherif's direct clinical feedback:
- **Zero Exhaustion:** Eliminates monotonous, wall-to-wall text boxes. Standardizes rapid, tactile interactions: single-tap choice chips, numeric steppers with +/- controls, smart clinical presets, and progressive disclosure for secondary details.
- **Visual Hierarchy & Contrast:** Small, muted labels (`11-12sp`, uppercase or medium tone) strictly separated from large, high-contrast clinical values (`15-18sp`, bold/semi-bold). Units are embedded inside values, never occupying standalone boxes.
- **Ergonomics & Touch Targets:** Every interactive element meets or exceeds Android's 48 dp touch target requirement. Buttons and chips wrap cleanly with zero vertical character splitting, even under large font scaling.
- **Safety & Field Completeness:** **Zero clinical fields are dropped.** Every existing field from the database models (`DemoPatient`, `DemoPreviousPregnancy`, `DemoPatientDating`, `DeliveryNoteSections`) is preserved, either immediately visible or reachable via 1-tap progressive disclosure ("Additional Details").
- **No Internal Jargon:** Eradicated internal words like "Codex" from all user-facing interfaces.

## 3. Recommended Options & Clinical Rationale

1. **Screen 1 (Obstetric History): Option A (Interactive Timeline & Stepper Hub)**  
   *Reason:* Obstetricians think chronologically about prior pregnancies. The visual timeline lets Dr. Sherif review years, birth routes, and outcomes at a glance without scrolling through repetitive cards. Direct G/P/A steppers eliminate keyboard typing completely during intake.

2. **Screen 2 (Previous Pregnancies Editor): Option A (Outcome-Driven Tap Matrix)**  
   *Reason:* In 95% of clinical encounters, the outcome dictates the rest of the form. Tapping `Live Birth - CS` instantly sets the outcome and birth method simultaneously; tapping `Miscarriage` immediately opens loss management chips without irrelevant birth weight fields.

3. **Screen 3 (Pregnancy Dating Engine): Option A (Multi-Method Comparator & Live Adjudicator)**  
   *Reason:* Resolving dating discrepancies is one of the most critical clinical tasks in the first trimester. Showing the LMP EDD side-by-side with the Ultrasound EDD and providing a clear, 1-tap "Use Scan as Agreed" button eliminates mental arithmetic and prevents redating errors.

4. **Screen 4 (Clinical Attention Card): Option A (Tiered Alert Badges with Tap-to-Expand)**  
   *Reason:* Prevents visual fatigue on patient lists and profile headers while ensuring that life-threatening vitals (Severe BP in crimson) are never missed or visually conflated with routine clinical notes (amber).

5. **Screen 5 (Delivery Plan Editor): Option A (Structured Protocol Form with Pre-Op Checklist)**  
   *Reason:* Solves the existing navigation bug cleanly by giving Dr. Sherif a dedicated, rapid delivery planning sheet. Standardizing blood bank precautions, target gestational age, and hospital fees into rapid chips ensures pre-operative safety with zero typing exhaustion.
