Revision B · Single Laboratory Investigation Mock

Streamlined, story-like single investigation entry correlated with the approved batch design (`rev-b/lab-batch.html`). Elimination of obsolete preset clutter ("Reactive", "Non-reactive", etc.) in favor of core clinical fields: Test, Value, Unit, Reference range, and Status.

Design Correlation & Optimization Record

  • Story-like Layout: Replaced the clunky numbered sections ("1. Laboratory Details & Date") with the sleek BatchInfoBar (Date · GA, Lab Name, Change ▾).
  • Elimination of Redundant Preset Options: Removed the hardcoded buttons for "Reactive", "Non-reactive", etc. Tests now cleanly present Test Name, Value, Unit, and Reference range.
  • Prominent Clinical Story Display: Entered values appear in a large, clear clinical box (e.g. 10.8 g/dL) accompanied by the diagnostic badge (Low ▼, Normal ✓, High ▲).
  • Urinalysis / Dipstick Specialization: Qualitative dipstick tests (e.g. Urine Protein) present big choice buttons (Normal, Trace, +, ++, +++) directly, matching Slice 4 of the batch mock.
  • Bottom Bar Alignment: Standard EditorPage bottom bar with Cancel, Save & add another, and primary Save investigation.
